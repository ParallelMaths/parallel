# Parallel Academy Year 8 Autumn Term Week 5 Assignment

These problems are designed to help you reflect on material from a recent tutorial - have your session materials at hand as the problems are closely based on them.

Please note:
* Problems are in order of increasing difficulty. **You are not expected to get full marks** and should not be alarmed by a low score. The most important thing is to try your best and to review the full solutions, working through any gaps.
* Your work must reflect your own best efforts and **you must not use AI or other tools** when working on the problems - please [review our AI policy here](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1jebSSqttUY8E7eLrHBE2GKJlP7QctCUQ/view?usp=sharing){target="_blank"}.
* **Set aside around 30 minutes** for the assignment (longer if you need it). Make sure you submit before the deadline stated on your Academy page.
* **Do not rush** - every problem deserves your attention. You should only leave an answer blank if you have really thought about the problem and are still stuck.
* For questions where you need to type in the answer, unless otherwise specified you may assume the answer is a whole number, and you should only enter the number itself.
* Before you click Submit at the end of the assignment, check that all of your answers have been entered correctly **(you will not be able to re-submit)**.
* **After you click Submit**, make sure you scroll down to see the correct answers and read the full written solutions.
