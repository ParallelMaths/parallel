## 1.	
::: problem id=1_1 marks=1
<!--- PMC 2021 (1) --->
I buy 10 plastic dinosaurs at 99p each and give the shopkeeper a £10 note.  

How much change should I receive?

* [ ] 1p
* [x] 10p
* [ ] £1
* [ ] £10
* [ ] £100
{.col-5}

---

Ten dinosaurs at 99p each will cost £9.90, so there will be 10p change.
:::


## 2.
::: problem id=2_1 marks=1
<!--- PMC 2021 (5) --->
![](/resources/primary-quiz-1/2-pie-chart.png){image align="right"} 
The pie chart on the right shows the numbers of pupils who answered the question “Is there life in Outer Space?" with a “yes” or “no”.  

There were 21 pupils who answered “yes”.  

How many pupils answered “no”?

* [ ] 4
* [ ] 5
* [ ] 6
* [x] 7
* [ ] 8
{.col-5}

---

We can see that `1/4` replied “no” and `3/4` “yes”.  

Since 21 replied “yes”, the number that replied “no” was 21 ÷ 3 = 7.
:::


## 3.
::: problem id=3_1 marks=1
<!--- PMC 2021 (11) --->
Jonathan the tortoise eats 20 g of salad leaves a day.  

The leaves are sold in 80 g bags which cost 90p.  

How much would it cost to feed Jonathan for 4 weeks?  

* [ ] £4.20
* [ ] £4.80
* [ ] £5.30
* [ ] £5.70
* [x] £6.30
{.col-5}

---

There are 28 days in four weeks, and each bag of salad will last four days.  

Therefore Jonathan will require seven bags over the four weeks.  

This will cost Jonathan or his owners 7 × 90p = £6.30.
:::


## 4.
<!--- 2018 (1) --->
::: problem id=4_1 marks=1
![](/resources/primary-quiz-1/4-balloons.png){image align="right"} 
As seen in the diagram, three darts are thrown at nine fixed balloons.  

If a balloon is hit it will burst and the dart continues in the same direction it had beforehand.  

How many balloons will not be hit by a dart? 

* [ ] 2
* [x] 3
* [ ] 4
* [ ] 5
* [ ] 6
{.col-5}

---

The highest dart hits the medium light-grey balloon then the large dark-grey balloon.  

The middle dart hits the small white balloon then the large black balloon.  

The lowest dart hits the extra-large white balloon then the small dark-grey.  

Only 3 balloons are not hit: the large dark-grey balloon (third from the left), the small black balloon (fourth from the right) and the large light-grey balloon (third from the right) are not hit.  
:::


## 5.
<!--- 2017 (15) --->
::: problem id=5_1 marks=1
Boris wants to increase his pocket money.  

To achieve this a fairy gives him three magic wands.  

He has to use every single one exactly once.  

![](/resources/primary-quiz-1/5-wands.png){image align="center"} 

In which order does he have to use the magic wands, in order to get the most money?  

![](/resources/primary-quiz-1/5-wands-options.png){image align="center"} 

* [ ] A
* [ ] B
* [ ] C
* [x] D
* [ ] E
{.col-5}

---

Doubling the amount of money will cause a greater increase if the amount has already been increased by 1.  

Therefore, Boris should select option D, adding 1, then doubling and then subtracting 1.  

This results in the final amount being 1 more than double Boris’ starting amount.  

All other options have the “+1” and “-1” wands next to each other, which cancels out their effects.  
:::
